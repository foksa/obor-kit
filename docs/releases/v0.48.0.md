- **Guard:** a bare `pi` from an agent's shell is denied (headless without a terminal); a kind may name whole multi-letter short options (`pi -np` is not `-p`).
- **Stalled workers:** an agent's detached helpers (pi's MCP server) no longer count as busy, and worker activity follows the session's recorded transcript wherever it lives.
- **OpenCode extension:** hooks get 15 s instead of 5 s (serve may be restarting), and a hook's warnings reach the console.
