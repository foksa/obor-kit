One verb for the lead's word to a task, and every task command by task id.

- **`obor resume <T-xx> ["<text>"] [--regate|--sendback]`**: replaces `obor answer` and `obor sendback`. Serve picks
  the action from the task's state: a worker's question gets the answer, a stalled or stopped worker or a drive the
  line, a green or reviewed task the sendback, a needs-you what its cause takes (a hold your decision, a failed step a
  retry, a gate failure a re-gate). Flags only where two actions fit: red past K takes `--regate` or `--sendback`, a
  Blocked report `--regate`. The `regate`/`reprompt`/`retry` words are gone: every needs-you line names the form to
  run.
- **Task ids only**: `obor drop <T-xx> "<why>" [--discard] [--force]` stops the task's worker and frees its lane;
  `obor drop <LANE>` and `obor sendback <LANE>` are gone.
